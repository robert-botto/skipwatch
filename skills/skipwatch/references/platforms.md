# Platforms

What each source kind needs and how it fails. Facts for X, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and
text posts come from the M3 spike (`docs/spikes/2026-10-m3-spike.md`, 2026-10-09, yt-dlp
2026.8.19, no cookies, treg CLI 0.22.0). Recorded fixtures live in `tests/fixtures/recorded/`.

treg call ids: `treg call` prints `treg: charged $<cost> · call id <32 hex>` on **stderr**,
never in the stdout JSON. Some calls add a second stderr line inviting `treg review <id> ...`.
Rate a call with `treg review <id> useful|partly|not_useful|not_sure --reason "<line>"`.
Every treg media URL below answered a plain ranged GET (no cookies, default UA) at once, but
TikTok and Instagram URLs expire: download right after the call, never cache the URL.

## Local files (M1)

Local files: sidecar captions `<stem>.vtt|.srt|.<lang>.vtt|.<lang>.srt`; no captions means Tier 1 is needed (Whisper, M2).

- Extensions are matched case-insensitively (`.MOV`, `.M4A`, `.webm` all work).
- A sidecar whose `<lang>` starts with `en` is preferred, then a plain `<stem>.vtt` or
  `<stem>.srt`. yt-dlp writes the `<stem>.<lang>.vtt` form.
- The original file is read in place; it is never copied into the work folder, moved or
  deleted.
- Paths with spaces and non-ASCII characters are normal (macOS names screen recordings
  `Screen Recording 2026-10-09 at 14.02.11.mov`); quote them in every command.
- Without captions and without Whisper the run degrades to frames only: steps can be
  `[on-screen]`, `[written]` or `[inferred]`, never `[spoken]`, and the review screen
  names the install command.

## YouTube (M2)

- Free route: works without cookies (G1, M2). yt-dlp needs a JS runtime (deno or node) for
  the YouTube challenge solver; `skipwatch doctor` reports it.
- Formats: separate video and audio streams up to 2160p; vertical Shorts too.
- Captions: manual and automatic tracks are common; json3 preferred, then vtt, srt.
- treg: `anyapi.youtube.video_transcript_full` (`{"url","captionKind":"any"}`, $0.003 seen)
  returns `segments[]` of `{startSeconds, endSeconds, text}` plus a flat `transcript`,
  `isAutoGenerated` and `language`. Hint only (issue 7). No media URL.
- Known failure modes: intermittent HTTP 403 or 429 from the CDN (retried once
  automatically); errors naming `--cookies` or a private video exit 4: print "Re-run with
  --cookies-from-browser <browser>".

### treg (Tier 3)

- Calls: `anyapi.youtube.video_transcript_full`, only when the video has no captions.
- Adds: the timed transcript as a hint only (`transcript_hint`, later `cross_check`); it is
  never the transcript and never a step source (R-ING-2, issue 7). No media fallback:
  treg has no YouTube media URL.

## Generic yt-dlp URLs (M2)

- Free route: archive.org and Wikimedia Commons work without cookies. Vimeo needs a login in
  yt-dlp 2026.8.19 ("The web client only works when logged-in"), so it exits 4 without
  `--cookies-from-browser`.
- Formats: often missing codec fields; Wikimedia offers manual captions in many languages.
- treg: none.
- Known failure modes: "Unsupported URL" exits 2 (try the text adapter for an article page).

## X (M3)

- Free route: **works without cookies** for a public video post (G2: metadata, formats and
  a 10 s partial download all fine; yt-dlp fetches a guest token itself).
- Fields: `description` is the full post text but newlines become spaces (the treg thread
  `text` keeps them); `title` is `"<name> - <text cut at about 50 chars>..."`, never use it
  as post text; `uploader_id` is the handle (`mattpocockuk`), `uploader` the display name.
- **`info["id"]` is the media id, not the post id** (G2: id `1498418617284767747`, post
  `1505892984658743300`). Take the post id from `webpage_url` (`/status/<id>`) for the
  canonical URL.
- A text post that only links or quotes another video post **returns that other post's
  video** (`x/link-to-video-post.info.json`: a reply whose text is "Previous tip:" plus a
  link yields a 76 s video with a different media id). The adapter cannot tell from
  `has_video` alone; compare durations or treat it as a normal video bundle and say so.
- Formats seen (short side): progressive `http-*` 270, 360, 720 and HLS video-only 270, 360,
  720 plus three HLS audio-only tracks. Max 720 for a 1280x720 upload.
- Captions: none (`subtitles` and `automatic_captions` empty).
- treg:
  - `anyapi.twitter.thread` ($0.00375): `output.data.tweets[]` with `authorHandle`, `text`
    (the media link stays a t.co link; continuation links are expanded), `isReply`, `inReplyToId`, `media[]` of
    `{type, url, videoUrl}`; G2 gave the post plus two author continuations
    (`threadLength: 3`). `videoUrl` on video.twimg.com: plain GET 206 `video/mp4`, no expiry
    parameter.
  - `treg.x.post.comments` (routed, header `X-Treg-Route-Max-Cost: 0.01`, served by
    `anyapi.x.post.comments`, $0.00045): `output.comments[]` with `authorHandle`, `text`,
    `id`, `url`; `output.next_cursor`; routing record in `_treg.served_by`, `_treg.tried[]`.
    Author replies to other accounts **are findable** by `authorHandle` (G2: 8 of 22 items
    are the author's, 6 of them replies to other people). Continuations also appear here.
  - `anyapi.twitter.tweet_transcript` ($0.0012): `output.data.transcript`, one untimed
    string, accurate for G2. Cross-check only.
- Known failure modes: text-only post exits 7, error line
  `ERROR: [twitter] <id>: No video could be found in this tweet`; print "This post has no
  video. Enable treg (Tier 3) to read text posts." Protected posts exit 4.

### treg (Tier 3)

- Calls: `anyapi.twitter.thread` (always), `treg.x.post.comments` (author replies) and
  `anyapi.twitter.tweet_transcript` (video posts); links in the thread are fetched.
- Adds: the author's later posts in `context.thread`, the author's own replies (never
  anyone else's) in `context.author_replies`, expanded links, and the untimed transcript
  as a hint for the cross-check. A text-only post becomes a `text` bundle from the
  thread. When yt-dlp is blocked, the thread's `videoUrl` is the media fallback.

## TikTok (M3)

- Free route: **works without cookies** (G3: metadata, formats, 10 s partial download).
- Fields: `description` is the caption with hashtags (same as treg `caption`); `uploader` is
  the handle (`github`), `channel` the display name, `uploader_id` a numeric id: build the
  author from `uploader`, not `uploader_id`. `title` is truncated.
- Formats seen (short side): h264 576 only; h265 (`bytevc1`) 576, 720, 1080; one `download`
  format without dimensions; an mp3 audio-only format. Each rendition appears twice (two CDN
  hosts). The 720 and 1080 renditions are h265 only.
- Captions: none.
- treg: `anyapi.tiktok.video_download` ($0.0008): `output.data` with `videoUrl`,
  `watermarkedUrl`, `caption`, `authorHandle`, `durationSeconds`. Both URLs: plain GET 206
  `video/mp4`. They expire: the expiry is a hex unix timestamp in the second path segment
  (not a query parameter), about a day ahead.
- Known failure modes: none seen logged out. `vm.tiktok.com` share links resolve through
  `TikTokVM` (not exercised in the spike).

### treg (Tier 3)

- Calls: `anyapi.tiktok.video_transcript_full` for video posts; `anyapi.tiktok.video_download`
  only as the media fallback.
- Adds: the transcript as a hint only (cross-check); a media fallback when yt-dlp is
  blocked (clean `videoUrl` first, then the watermarked one), downloaded at once because
  the URL expires.

## Instagram (M3)

- Free route: **works without cookies** for G4 (metadata, formats, 10 s partial download).
  Logged out, only recent reels of a profile are reachable; older ones may need cookies.
- Fields: `description` is the full caption; `title` is `"Video by <handle>"`, never post
  text; `channel` is the handle (`github`), `uploader` the display name, `uploader_id`
  numeric; `duration` is **null** logged out.
- Formats seen (short side): DASH video-only vp9 720 and 1080, one m4a audio track, and three
  progressive formats `1`, `2`, `3` with no width or height. The spike's `bv*[height<=1080]+ba/b`
  picked progressive `3`.
- Captions: none.
- treg: `anyapi.instagram.post.detail` ($0.0008): `output.data` with `videoUrl`,
  `displayUrl`, `owner`, `shortcode`, `type`, `likes`. **No caption text field and no
  duration.** `videoUrl`: plain GET 206 `video/mp4`; expires (query parameter `oe`, hex unix
  time).
- Known failure modes: login wall exits 4; print "Instagram often needs a login. Re-run with
  --cookies-from-browser <browser>."

### treg (Tier 3)

- Calls: `anyapi.instagram.post.detail`, only as the media fallback.
- Adds: a media fallback when yt-dlp is blocked (`videoUrl`, downloaded at once), and
  never for post text: the call returns no caption, so Instagram text always comes from the free
  route (spike decision 3).

## Facebook (M3)

- Free route: **works without cookies** for a public organisation page video (gov.sg,
  "What to do during the haze?", 65 s; metadata, formats, 10 s partial download).
- Fields: `description` is the post text; `title` the video title; `uploader` the page name;
  `uploader_id` numeric; `channel` null.
- Formats seen (short side): DASH video-only vp9 360, 540, 720, 1080; one m4a audio track;
  progressive `sd` and `hd` with no width or height.
- Captions: none.
- treg: `anyapi.facebook.post.detail` ($0.0012): `output.data` with `text`, `authorName`,
  `createdUtc`, counts. **No media URL**: `videoId` null, `image` empty, `durationSeconds` 0.
  treg cannot be a media fallback for Facebook.
- Known failure modes: page listings such as `/<page>/videos` are "Unsupported URL"
  (exit 2): ask for one video's URL. Login walls (not seen for a public page) exit 4 with
  the cookie flag.

### treg (Tier 3)

- Calls: `anyapi.facebook.post.detail`, only when the free route found no post text.
- Adds: the post text in that case, and never a media fallback: the call returns no media URL
  (spike decision 4), so never offer `--provider treg` for a Facebook download failure.

## Text posts (M3)

- X text-only post: yt-dlp exits with the "No video could be found" line above (exit 7 on the
  free route). With treg, `anyapi.twitter.thread` returns the text and continuations.
- Other pages: fetched by `links.fetch_link`; under 200 words exits 7.
- A post that links a video post is not a text post to yt-dlp (see X above).
